FreeFlow

FreeFlow is a hold-to-talk dictation app for Windows. Hold a key, speak, and clean, polished text lands wherever your cursor is: an email, a Word document, your browser, a chat window. It tidies filler words and mishearings automatically. It understands whatever language you speak, English, Persian, Spanish, Arabic, Turkish, French and many more, and writes it back in that same language.

Install

Five short steps to get started.

1. Download the installer.
Download FreeFlow-Setup.exe. You will find it as the file attached to this lesson, or at the link provided.

2. Run it.
Double-click FreeFlow-Setup.exe. Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" screen. This is normal: it happens because the app is new and not yet code-signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". It is safe.

3. Install.
The installer runs. No administrator rights are needed. Tick "Start FreeFlow automatically when I log in" if you want it always ready. Click through to Finish.

4. Add your free Groq key.
On first launch, a small window asks for a Groq API key. This is what powers the transcription.

  1. Go to console.groq.com.
  2. Sign up for a free account.
  3. Create an API key.
  4. Copy the key.
  5. Paste it into FreeFlow and click Save.
Two minutes, no card needed. Getting a Groq key takes about two minutes and needs no credit card. The key is stored privately on your own computer. It is only ever sent to Groq when you dictate.

5. Say hello.
A short welcome window explains the basics. A tray icon and a small floating gadget appear at the bottom-right of your screen. You are ready to go.

How to use it

Once FreeFlow is installed, here is the one motion you will use most.

Hold, speak, release

Select where you want the text to go. Click into an email, a document, or a chat. Hold Alt + 1 together, speak your sentence, then let go.

A moment later, the cleaned text appears at your cursor. There is a short cloud round-trip while this happens, about one to three seconds. The gadget shows a processing state and a running time, so you always know it is working.

Speak naturally

Full stops and commas are added for you. Filler words are removed. Say "new line" or "new paragraph" to add a line break.

Writing modes

FreeFlow adjusts its tone to match the app you are typing into. This happens automatically, with no extra effort from you.

ModeUsed inWhat you get
PolishedEmails, WordClear and correct
Brand voiceLinkedIn, NotionWarm and on-brand
NoteSlack, Teams, WhatsApp, TelegramShort and casual
PromptAI chat windowsLiteral, unpolished
RawCode editorsExact words, no cleanup

You can also lock a mode from the gadget if you prefer.

Languages and translate

By default, FreeFlow detects your language automatically and writes it back in the same language. If you would rather choose yourself, click the language pill on the gadget to lock a language: English, Farsi, or Auto.

Translate mode is a toggle on the gadget. Switch it on, and FreeFlow writes out your speech in British English, whatever language you spoke.

Hands-free session mode

Double-tap Alt + 1 to keep the microphone open. Speak in bursts, without holding the key down each time. Double-tap again to stop. This suits longer pieces of writing.

Voice editing: change text you have already written

Select any text in any app. Hold Alt + 1 and say an instruction starting with "edit this", "rewrite this", or "edit selection". FreeFlow rewrites the selected text in place.

Examples:

  • "Edit this: make it more formal."
  • "Rewrite this as bullet points."
  • "Edit this: shorten it."
  • "Edit this: translate to Farsi."

Changed your mind? Say "undo paste" within two minutes to reverse it.

Meeting notes

From the tray icon, choose "Meeting notes: start". FreeFlow records your microphone and transcribes in the background while you talk.

When you are done, choose "Meeting notes: stop". FreeFlow writes and opens a document: a summary first (key points, decisions, action items), then the full transcript.

This version records your microphone only.

If something looks wrong

Most hiccups have a quick fix.

ProblemWhat to do
Nothing appearsMake sure you held Alt + 1 while speaking, then released it. Check the tray icon is running.
Wrong languageClick the gadget's language pill and lock your language.
A strange or garbled sentenceRare. FreeFlow falls back to your raw words. Delete it, try again, and speak a little more slowly.
It feels slowThe first use in any session can be slower, while it warms up.
